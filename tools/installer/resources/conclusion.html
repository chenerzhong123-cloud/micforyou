下一步 / Next steps

若安装器要求重启，请先重启 Mac，再打开 /Applications/MicForYou.app，从菜单中的设置注册声音。

If prompted, restart your Mac, then open /Applications/MicForYou.app and register your voice in Settings.

从旧 ZIP 升级时，~/Applications 可能还有旧版 App；请启动 /Applications 中的新版本。个人数据保存在 ~/Library/Application Support/MicForYou。

An older ZIP app may remain in ~/Applications; launch the new app from /Applications. Personal data remains in ~/Library/Application Support/MicForYou.

音频组件修复可在 App 设置中离线运行，无需重新下载。Offline audio-component repair is available in the app settings.
